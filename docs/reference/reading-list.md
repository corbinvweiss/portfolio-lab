# Reading list

Grouped by the week that first needs each source. Read the primary items before the
week's brief; the rest are for the lesson's Reading section.

## Foundations (weeks 1-4)

- Campbell, *Financial Decisions and Markets* (2018), chapters 1-4: utility, static
  portfolio choice, CAPM, the stochastic discount factor. Primary for weeks 3-4.
- MIT OCW 15.401 Finance Theory I (Lo): PV relations, portfolio theory, CAPM.
  https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/15-401-finance-theory-i-fall-2008/
- Ledoit and Wolf, "Honey, I Shrunk the Sample Covariance Matrix" (JPM 2004). Primary
  for week 4.
- Asness, Frazzini and Pedersen, "Leverage Aversion and Risk Parity" (FAJ 2012).
  https://www.aqr.com/-/media/AQR/Documents/Insights/Journal-Article/Leverage-Aversion-and-Risk-Parity.pdf

## Rigor (weeks 5-7)

- Elton, Gruber and Blake, "Survivor Bias and Mutual Fund Performance" (RFS 1996).
  https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=1299448
- Brown, Goetzmann, Ibbotson and Ross, "Survivorship Bias in Performance Studies"
  (RFS 1992). https://terpconnect.umd.edu/~wermers/ftpsite/FAME/Brown_Goetzmann_Ibbotson_Ross.pdf
- Asness, Frazzini, Israel and Moskowitz, "Fact, Fiction and Momentum Investing" (JPM 2014).
  https://www.aqr.com/-/media/AQR/Documents/Journal-Articles/JPM-Fact-Fiction-and-Momentum-Investing.pdf
- Asness, Moskowitz and Pedersen, "Value and Momentum Everywhere" (JF 2013).
  https://pages.stern.nyu.edu/~lpederse/papers/ValMomEverywhere.pdf
- Lopez de Prado, *Advances in Financial Machine Learning* (2018), chapters 7, 11, 12, 14.
- Bailey and Lopez de Prado, "The Deflated Sharpe Ratio" (JPM 2014).
  https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=2460551
- Bailey, Borwein, Lopez de Prado and Zhu, "The Probability of Backtest Overfitting"
  (J. Comp. Finance 2015). https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=2326253
- Harvey and Liu, "Backtesting" (JPM 2015).
  https://people.duke.edu/~charvey/Research/Published_Papers/P120_Backtesting.PDF
- Harvey, Liu and Zhu, "...and the Cross-Section of Expected Returns" (RFS 2016).
  https://people.duke.edu/~charvey/Research/Published_Papers/P118_and_the_cross.PDF
- Ken French Data Library (factor data).
  https://mba.tuck.dartmouth.edu/pages/faculty/ken.french/data_library.html

## Instruments and financing (weeks 8-10)

- MIT OCW 15.450 Analytics of Finance: risk-neutral pricing, Black-Scholes, Merton
  allocation. https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/15-450-analytics-of-finance-fall-2010/
- Hull, *Options, Futures and Other Derivatives*, chapters on forward pricing, put-call
  parity, Black-Scholes, greeks.
- Vanguard, "Going Global with Bonds" (currency hedging of foreign bonds).
  https://www.vanguard.co.uk/professional/research/going-global-with-bonds
- Vanguard, "Global Equity Investing: Diversification and Sizing".

## Portfolio management (weeks 11-12)

- Swensen, *Unconventional Success* (2005), then *Pioneering Portfolio Management*
  (2009). Read in that order.
- Dimmock, Wang and Yang, "The Endowment Model and Modern Portfolio Theory" (NBER
  w25559). https://www.nber.org/system/files/working_papers/w25559/w25559.pdf
- Campbell and Viceira, *Strategic Asset Allocation* (2002); NBER summary:
  https://www.nber.org/reporter/fall-2000/strategic-asset-allocation-portfolio-choice-long-term-investors
- Brinson, Hood and Beebower, "Determinants of Portfolio Performance" (FAJ 1986), and
  Ibbotson and Kaplan's correction of the "90%" reading.
- CFA Level III portfolio management learning outcomes (asset allocation, IPS,
  performance evaluation).
- Vanguard, "Best Practices for Portfolio Rebalancing"; "Advisor's Alpha"; "Asset
  Location for Equity"; "Principles for Investing Success".
  https://corporate.vanguard.com/content/dam/corp/research/pdf/vanguards_principles_for_investing_success.pdf
- Morningstar, "Mind the Gap 2025"; Kinnel, "Fund Fees Predict Future Success or Failure".
  https://www.morningstar.com/funds/fund-fees-predict-future-success-or-failure
- Sharpe, "The Arithmetic of Active Management" (1991).
  https://web.stanford.edu/~wfsharpe/art/active/active.htm
- Malkiel, *A Random Walk Down Wall Street*. Background reading for the whole course.

## Survey courses

- Shiller, ECON 252 Financial Markets (Open Yale). https://oyc.yale.edu/economics/econ-252
- Princeton FIN 501 (Brunnermeier) syllabus: no-arbitrage, mean-variance, CAPM, derivatives.
  https://www.princeton.edu/~markus/teaching/Fin501/00Syllabus_Fin501.pdf
